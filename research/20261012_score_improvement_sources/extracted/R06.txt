Japan-U.S.-France Multilateral Exercise 
(Pacific Steller) 
 
 
The JMSDF will conduct a multilateral exercise with the United States Navy (USN) and the French 
Navy as described below in order to strengthen cooperation to realize a “Free and Open Indo-Pacific”. 
 
1. Objectives 
(1) To improve the JMSDF’s tactical capabilities 
(2) To strengthen cooperation with participating navies 
 
2. Period 
February 10 - 18 
 
3. Exercise Area 
Eastern sea and airspace of the Philippines 
 
4. Participating units 
(1) JMSDF: JS KAGA, JS AKIZUKI, P-3C 
(2) USN: USS CARL VINSON, USS PRINCETON, USS STERETT, USS WILLIAM P. LAWRENCE, 
        P-8A 
(3) French Navy: FS CHARLES DE GAULLE, FS ALSACE, FS FORBIN, FS PROVENCE, 
              FS JACQUES CHEVALLIER, ATLANTIC 
 
5. Type of Exercise 
Tactical Exercise ( Anti-Air Warfare, Anti-Submarine Warfare, LINKEX, Cross Deck etc.), and 
PHOTOEX.
